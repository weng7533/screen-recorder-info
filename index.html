Screen Recorder

Screen Recorder is a personal recording app. With your permission, it records screen video and supported app audio and saves finished recordings privately to your Google Drive.

Recording starts only after you choose to start and approve screen capture. You can stop recording at any time. Saved recordings remain under your control in Google Drive.

The uploader requests access only to files it creates or that you explicitly allow it to use.

Privacy policy

Personal-use terms

This app is for personal use. Choose what to record, obtain any permissions needed for that recording, and manage or delete your saved recordings in Google Drive. You can revoke the uploader's access in your Google account settings.
