Screen Recorder privacy policy

Updated October 5, 2026. This app is for personal use.

With your permission, Screen Recorder captures screen video and supported app audio. Recordings are securely transmitted for processing and uploaded to a private folder in your Google Drive. A local copy is retained until the cloud upload is verified.

The uploader uses Google Drive access to create recording files and folders, upload recordings, and check their size and checksum. It reads your account email and storage quota to confirm the intended account and available storage. Its Google permission is limited to files it creates or files you explicitly allow it to use.

Recordings and Google account data are not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with other people. Google processes files stored in Drive under its own privacy policy. Authorization credentials are stored securely and are not included in the phone app.

You can stop recording, delete saved files in Google Drive, and revoke the uploader's access in your Google account settings. Upload failures retain the local recording for a later retry.
